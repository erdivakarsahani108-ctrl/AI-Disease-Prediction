# Medical Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 2026

## This is NOT a Medical Diagnosis System

The AI-Based Disease Prediction & Intelligent Health Assistant ("this
application") is an **educational and informational Data Science / AI
project**. It is built for academic demonstration purposes as a Final Year
B.Tech project.

- Predictions are the statistical output of a machine learning model
  trained on a limited, partly synthetic dataset. **Model confidence is
  not a medically validated probability.**
- This application does **not** diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any
  disease.
- This application does **not** prescribe medicine, dosage, or treatment.
- The "Risk Indicator" is an AI-assisted heuristic, **not** a clinical
  risk score.
- The Emergency/Red-Flag detector is a best-effort safety layer. It **may
  miss genuine emergencies** and must never be relied upon in place of
  professional or emergency medical care.

## Always Consult a Professional

If you are experiencing symptoms, please consult a qualified healthcare
professional. If you believe you are having a medical emergency, contact
your local emergency services immediately - do not wait for or rely on
this application.
